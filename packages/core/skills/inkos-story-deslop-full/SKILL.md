# Whole-chapter semantic polish

Polish the entire chapter. Change only expression, rhythm, and paragraph breathing. Do not change facts, plot conclusions, names, or causality.

Allowed: word choice, sentence order, punctuation rhythm, concrete action instead of summary.
Forbidden: adding or deleting scenes, new dialogue that changes events, new plot, viewpoint shifts, banned-word search-and-replace.

Diagnose by function and effect, not by counting stock phrases. Distinguish a real defect from a legitimate voice choice.

Preserve plot facts, viewpoint, character voice, evidence, pacing function, and strong original lines.
Respond in the user's language.
